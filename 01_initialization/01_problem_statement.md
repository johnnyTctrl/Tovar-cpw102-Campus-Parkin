# Problem Statement

## Campus Parking

Students and visitors who park on campus need a simple way to determine how much their parking will cost.

At the moment, drivers have to calculate the cost themselves. This can be inconvenient and may lead to mistakes, especially when parking for partial hours.

Campus staff would like a small program that can calculate the expected parking cost for a driver.

### Current Parking Policy

- Parking costs **$2.00 per hour**.
- Drivers may park for any number of hours, including partial hours.

### The Need

The program should make it easier for drivers to estimate their parking cost before or during a parking session.

The solution should:

- Be simple to use from a computer terminal.
- Clearly communicate the calculated parking cost.
- Keep the parking calculation organized so it can be reused if the program is expanded later.
